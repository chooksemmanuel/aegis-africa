# Competition recognition

## Confirmed result

Aegis Africa was developed as a solo product concept and pitch for the **Growth Summit 2026 Pitch Competition** in March 2026.

A certificate reviewed during repository preparation states that **Emmanuel Ihejiamaizu received 4th place** in the competition. It identifies the organizers as:

- Google Developer Group, Conestoga College
- IT Club
- Conestoga Students Inc.

## What this recognition means

The result confirms participation and placement in the competition. It does not establish that:

- The proposed product was built or deployed at that time.
- Judges validated customer demand, technical accuracy, market size, or pricing.
- The project had users, revenue, partners, pilots, or operational results.
- The proposed AI, WhatsApp, email, or fintech features existed.

## Public evidence approach

The original certificate contains personal identifying information. It is not included in this initial public repository. A future public asset may be added only after the founder decides what evidence should be published and confirms that unnecessary metadata or correspondence is removed.

Any future wording should remain factual: "Placed 4th in the Growth Summit 2026 Pitch Competition." It should not be expanded into claims of product validation or commercial traction.
