## Definitions

- **Contacted:** A specific outreach message was sent. This does not mean a conversation occurred.
- **Completed conversation:** A substantive discussion took place and notes were recorded.
- **Planned contact:** A person or organization category has been identified, but no outreach has been sent.
- **Validated finding:** A conclusion supported by a documented research method and multiple relevant observations. A single opinion is not treated as validated demand.
- **Partnership:** A formal relationship that both parties have agreed can be described publicly. Outreach, a meeting, or interest is not a partnership.

## Assumptions still requiring validation

1. Message-based fraud is a sufficiently frequent and costly problem for the selected business segment.
2. Business owners want a separate security tool rather than guidance from existing banks, platforms, or associations.
3. A WhatsApp-centered interaction is both technically feasible and preferred by users.
4. A rule-based warning can improve decisions without creating excessive false alarms.
5. Users can understand and act on the explanations provided.
6. A subscription price proposed in the pitch would be acceptable.
7. A business-to-business-to-customer distribution model is feasible.
8. Region-specific data would improve detection enough to justify collection and model development.
9. The same product approach can serve multiple countries, languages, sectors, and payment systems.

## Minimum details needed to add a real entry

Before adding any prior contact, confirm:

- The person's non-identifying category.
- The country or market, at a level safe to publish.
- Whether outreach was sent or a conversation actually occurred.
- The approximate month.
- The high-level subject discussed.
- Whether notes exist.
- Whether the person agreed to any public quotation or identification.
- Which details must remain private.

Do not publish names, email addresses, phone numbers, private messages, employer-sensitive information, or quotations without explicit permission.
