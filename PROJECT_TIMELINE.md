# Project timeline

This timeline separates confirmed project activity from future work. It is intentionally conservative and should be updated only when supporting evidence exists.

## March 2026 - original concept and competition

- Aegis Africa was developed as a product concept and solo pitch for the Growth Summit 2026 Pitch Competition.
- The pitch described a possible future mobile-first cybersecurity service for African small businesses, including a WhatsApp security bot, email-fraud monitoring, and short security-awareness lessons.
- The pitch received 4th place in the competition.
- The pitch deck, written proposal, HTML presentation, pitch script, and certificate were reviewed before this repository was organized.
- Product, market, pricing, competitor, and statistical statements in those materials are historical pitch claims, not proof of implementation or validation.

## After the competition - documentation and limited outreach

- The founder continued documenting the concept.
- The founder stated that some early contacts were made to better understand the market.
- No publishable list of contacts, completed conversations, dates, or findings was supplied during the initial repository build.
- The repository therefore records no interview count, quotation, insight, validation result, or partnership claim.

## Spring-Summer 2026 - pause

- Active product development did not continue immediately while the founder focused on academic projects.
- The founder also described a short personal and health-related recovery period.
- This period should not be represented as development, customer research, traction, or technical progress.

## August 2026 - Phase 0 restart

- The original materials were reviewed for contradictions and unsupported claims.
- A public-facing repository structure was created.
- A small Streamlit prototype was built using local, explainable rules.
- Synthetic examples and automated tests were added.
- No AI model, production integration, customer deployment, or live threat-intelligence service was added.

## Next evidence gates

The timeline should be updated only after one or more of the following occurs:

1. A structured market conversation is completed and recorded without personal information.
2. A primary use case is selected based on evidence.
3. A limited MVP requirement is documented.
4. A test dataset is collected with consent and anonymization.
5. Accuracy and false-positive results are measured and reproducible.
6. An official platform integration is implemented under its policies.
7. A pilot relationship is formally agreed and can be publicly disclosed.
